About Me

I’m Amir Rahman, an AI Engineer focused on building computer vision and machine learning systems for real-world operational environments, particularly in surveillance, automation, and data-driven decision systems.

My work spans computer vision, deep learning, and applied machine learning, with an emphasis on deploying models that operate under real-world constraints such as noise, scale, and limited data quality. I am especially interested in building systems that improve situational awareness and decision-making through intelligent perception.

I graduated from Singapore Polytechnic with a Diploma in Applied AI & Analytics, where I developed a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, and software engineering.
